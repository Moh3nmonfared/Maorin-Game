The Last Gate

Above the clouds stands the final bastion of the celestial realm — a citadel of crystal and gold, guarded by ancient dragons. Beyond the horizon, a storm of void-touched legions gathers under a violet sky.

You are a Lord of the Gate. Raise towers, command heroes, and hold the bridge as the Void rises to claim the heavens.

Core Mechanics

Build the Defense

Place towers on sacred sigils along winding sky roads. Upgrade, merge and specialize each one.

Command Heroes

Deploy one of nine classes onto the battlefield and unleash ultimate abilities at the critical moment.

Master the Elements

Fire, Ice, Nature, Light and Void counter each other. Read each wave and adapt your lineup.

Nine Character Classes

Eleven Legendary Towers

Each tower is forged from crystal, bound to an element, and built to break the Void.

The Elemental Wheel

Hover or tap a gem to awaken its power.

Light

The radiance of the Gate itself. Light towers purify void corruption and empower allies.
